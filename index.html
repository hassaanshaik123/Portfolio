A little about me

Some context

I'm Muhammad Hassaan, currently pursuing my MBA at Masters' Union School of Business.

I've always been interested in building things, figuring out how businesses work, and helping people find their way forward. I've spent time building ventures from 0 to 1, mentoring people, and exploring different sides of business.

One thing I'm learning along the way is that sales can be genuinely rewarding. Not when you're trying to convince someone to buy something, but when you listen closely, understand what they actually need, and help them find the right solution.

That's the side of sales I connect with most. And it's what I'm working on getting better at every day.

This website is a little space to share what I've been working on, what I've learned, and where I'm headed next.
