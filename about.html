I'm Muhammad Hassaan — founder of 3 ventures, MBA candidate at Masters' Union, and currently building Raah.AI. Everything I've shipped runs on one principle: problem first, evidence always.

01The preface

Most people collect titles. I collect reps at the bottleneck. Campus content that died every batch, skills that never became income, students guessing their careers — each venture I've founded started by naming a real bottleneck and building a measurable system around it.

That's also how I think about product management: not roadmaps and ceremony, but diagnosis → system → evidence. The ventures are my case studies; the operating system underneath them is the product I keep refining.

03How I operate

Four rules I've earned the expensive way:

  • Diagnose before prescribing — no assessment, no roadmap. Opinion is not evidence.
  • Systems beat vibes — communities, content, products: everything runs on a loop you can instrument.
  • Evidence over adjectives — if a number can't move, the story doesn't count.
  • Compounding over spikes — the curve stays flat, then bends. Trust the loop.
