I'm Muhammad Hassaan Shaik — an MBA candidate at Masters' Union who has spent the last four years doing the thing, not just studying it: founding communities, running a content agency, selling in EdTech, and building an AI product on the side.

The preface

Most people collect titles. I collect reps at the bottleneck. Campus content that died every batch, skills that never became income, students guessing their careers — every venture I've run started by naming a real problem and building a measurable system around it.

Somewhere along the way I discovered the part I love most isn't just building — it's selling what I build. Consultative selling at an EdTech taught me that revenue is the most honest feedback loop there is. That's the direction I'm heading after my MBA: sales, GTM and business development, with a builder's hands and an operator's discipline.
