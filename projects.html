01

Raah.AI — AI Career Diagnosis Platform

Founder · Product·2026 — Present · Gurugram

Most students don't choose careers — they inherit them from peer pressure, LinkedIn aesthetics and one-off advice. Raah.AI flips that: it diagnoses where a student actually stands before recommending where they could go.

The full story — role, contribution, result

Role

  • Builder & no-code developer — I own the product end to end.
  • Concept, user research, workflow design and build.

Contribution

  • Designed the core workflow: qualifications, interests and long-term goals → personalised AI recommendation.
  • Simulation layer that assesses decision-making and career preferences before it prescribes.
  • Market research done directly with students — the product is built on what they said, not what I assumed.

Result

  • MVP in active build; pilot cohort planned with Masters' Union students.
  • Every user test sharpens the same claim: careers decided on evidence, not vibes.

02

LYVO — Honest Conversations with Life Coaches

Founder·Ideation stage

A whole generation is tired of being lonely — and doesn't need a clinical diagnosis to deserve a real conversation. LYVO connects lonely or mentally struggling Gen Z with India's renowned life coaches for short, honest conversational sessions — someone who will actually listen, without the therapy-room weight.

The full story — role, contribution, result

Role

  • Founder — shaping the concept, the promise and the operating model.

Contribution

  • Defined the positioning: conversational, not clinical — no forms-first coldness, just a person who listens.
  • Mapping the coach side: renowned life coaches willing to give short, focused sessions.
  • Designing the ask: what a lonely 20-year-old actually needs in a first conversation.

Result

  • Ideation stage — promise defined, coach conversations beginning.
  • Sharing the build log openly as it takes shape.
